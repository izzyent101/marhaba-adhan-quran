# Cloud background footage

“Clouds (time lapse)” by Editor (https://www.youtube.com/user/Editor).
Original: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Clouds_(time_lapse).webm
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Adaptations: encoded to 720p H.264 without audio, two-second seam blend for looping,
quarter-speed playback, daytime/dawn/dusk color and opacity blending in the app.
No endorsement is implied. The attribution is also accessible in Display & updates.

Stars are generated by the app and animated individually; no translated still images are used.
